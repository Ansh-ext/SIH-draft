SOP-14: Pressure Relief Valve Inspection and Approval Procedure

Scope: This procedure applies to all pressure relief valves (PRVs) installed
on process vessels and piping in the crude distillation and hydrocracking
units.

Inspection frequency: PRVs must be inspected every 12 months, or every 6
months for valves in hydrogen or high-H2S service.

Approval note requirements: Any inspection report noting valve seat leakage,
corrosion on the valve body, or a set-pressure deviation greater than 3%
from nameplate rating must be escalated to the Rotating Equipment section
head for review before the valve is returned to service. The approval note
must include: valve tag number, last inspection date, observed deviation,
inspector's recommendation, and section head sign-off.

Deviations exceeding 5% require the valve to be pulled and bench-tested
before any approval note can be issued.

Reference: API 527, API 576.
