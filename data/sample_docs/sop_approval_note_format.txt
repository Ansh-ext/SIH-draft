SOP-02: Standard Approval Note Format

All approval notes generated from inspection findings must follow this
structure:

1. Header: equipment tag, unit, date of inspection, inspector name.
2. Findings summary: 2-4 sentences describing what was observed.
3. Reference SOPs: list every SOP consulted, with section numbers.
4. Deviation assessment: state whether the finding is within tolerance,
   requires monitoring, or requires escalation.
5. Recommendation: one clear action - return to service, monitor, escalate,
   or pull for bench testing.
6. Sign-off block: preparer, reviewer, and approver fields.

Approval notes are stored in the department's SOP-compliant archive and
must be retrievable by equipment tag number for at least 7 years.
